Your First Sunday

What to Expect

We want you to feel comfortable from the moment you arrive. No pressure, no performance, no awkwardness.

🎵

Live Worship

Sincere, modern music that invites rather than performs. You're free to participate however feels natural to you.

📖

A Real Message

Honest, practical teaching from the Bible. In English — with Russian translation available via earpiece.

☕

Coffee & Connection

After the service, stay for coffee. People genuinely want to meet you — no awkward pressure, just real conversation.

👶

Kids Are Welcome

KidsZone runs during the Sunday service — a safe, fun environment for children ages 0–12.

🚗

Free Parking

Free parking is available on-site. Our greeters will help you find your way from the moment you arrive.

⏱

About 90 Minutes

Services run approximately 90 minutes — just enough time to worship, learn, and connect without rushing.

Practical Info

A Few Things to Know

👕

Dress Code

There isn't one. Come in jeans, come in a suit, come as you are. We care about you — not what you're wearing.

🌐

Language

Services are bilingual. Russian translation via earpiece is available at the welcome desk every Sunday.

📍

Arrival

Aim to arrive 10 minutes early. Our greeters at the entrance will help you find your seat and answer any questions.

💳

Offering

There's no pressure or spotlight on giving. Offering is a personal response to God — completely voluntary for guests.
