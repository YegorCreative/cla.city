Discipleship

Jesus School

A structured discipleship track that takes you from the foundations of faith to a life of meaningful ministry. Open to all — no prior knowledge required.

01

Foundations of Faith

The essentials of Christian belief — who God is, what Jesus did, and how to begin a life of real faith. Ideal for new believers and seekers.

6 weeks · Beginner
02

Kingdom Discipleship

Discover what it means to live as a citizen of God's Kingdom — in your family, workplace, relationships, and community.

8 weeks · Intermediate
03

Spirit-Filled Life

An honest, biblical exploration of the Holy Spirit, spiritual gifts, prayer, and how to walk in the power God intends for every believer.

6 weeks · Intermediate
04

Practical Ministry

Training for those who feel called to serve — covering leadership principles, pastoral care, preaching basics, and team dynamics.

8 weeks · Advanced
05

Biblical Counseling

Equip yourself to walk alongside others through grief, addiction, anxiety, and relational conflict using Scripture-based principles.

10 weeks · Advanced
06

Evangelism & Missions

Learn how to share your faith naturally and effectively — whether in daily conversations, outreach events, or cross-cultural mission fields.

6 weeks · All levels

Reading Plan

Bible in One Year

Reading the entire Bible in a year is one of the most transformative habits a believer can build. Our guided plan pairs Old and New Testament passages daily, with short reflections written by Pastor David and the Church LA team.

Available on our website in Russian and English. Join our community in this habit — and discover the Bible isn't a chore. It's a conversation with God.
