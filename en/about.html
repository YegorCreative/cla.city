Our Story

Built on Faith,
Rooted in Community

A bilingual church that grew from a small living room gathering into a family spanning two cultures — and still growing.

Our Mission

Love God. Love People. Change the City.

Church LA exists to be a Spirit-led, Bible-based community where people encounter the living God, grow in genuine faith, and step into their God-given purpose.

We believe the church is not a building or a Sunday routine — it is a family. And families make room for everyone: the doubter, the seeker, the longtime believer, and the person walking in off the street for the first time.

We hold services in both English and Russian because we believe unity across cultures reflects the Kingdom of God.

Core Beliefs

What We Believe

We hold to the historic Christian faith as revealed in Scripture. Here are the convictions that shape everything we do.

1

The Bible

The Scriptures are the inspired, authoritative Word of God — our final guide for faith and life.

2

The Trinity

There is one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

3

Salvation by Grace

We are saved by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone — not by works, tradition, or personal merit.

4

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the present-day work of the Holy Spirit — filling believers, empowering ministry, and transforming lives.

5

Baptism & Communion

Water baptism is an outward declaration of inward faith. Communion is a regular act of remembrance and covenant.

6

The Church

The local church is the primary expression of God's Kingdom on earth — a community called to worship, grow, serve, and send.

7

Prayer

We believe prayer is not a religious formality but a living conversation with a God who hears and responds.

8

The Return of Christ

We believe in the literal, bodily return of Jesus Christ and the ultimate restoration of all things.
