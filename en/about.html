Who We Are

A Russian-speaking church in Los Angeles

Our Story

Getting to Know Our Story

Leonid and Marina Malko began leading Church LA in 2021.

Today, we continue to welcome Russian-speaking people and families in North Hollywood and across Los Angeles.

Meet the Pastors

Pastors Leonid and Marina Malko

Leonid and Marina Malko lead the church and help people feel welcome.

Pastor Leonid Malko

Lead Pastor

Leonid has been CLA’s senior pastor since 2021. The church is affiliated with NHFC (LA International Christian Center).

Since his youth, he has helped start churches, trained church workers, and served in missions. He worked in Armenia and Siberia, led a missionary training center in Moscow, and served as a pastor in Los Angeles.

He also helps train church workers through the Missionary Training Center (mtc.today).

Pastor Marina Malko

Co-Pastor · Women's Group

Marina is CLA’s co-pastor and leads the women’s group. She was ordained in Los Angeles in 2021.

She and Leonid have helped start churches and served in missions for many years. Her experience includes children’s activities, Christian education, and women’s gatherings, with Bible study and missionary training in Sweden and Moscow.

Marina shares messages from the Bible, prays with people, and cares for families. She wants every woman and family to find friendship, support, and a place to explore faith at CLA.

Core Beliefs

What We Believe

We hold to biblical Christian faith as revealed in Scripture. It shapes how we live and care for one another.

1

The Bible

Scripture is the inspired, authoritative Word of God and the church's authority for faith and life.

2

Jesus Christ

Jesus Christ is the only way to salvation.

3

The Trinity

There is one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

4

Salvation by Grace

Salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, not by works, tradition, or personal merit.

5

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the present work of the Holy Spirit, who fills believers and empowers ministry.

6

Baptism & Communion

Water baptism is an outward declaration of inward faith. Communion is a regular act of remembrance and covenant.

7

The Church

The local church is a community called to worship, grow, serve, and send.

8

Prayer

Prayer is central to the life of the church.
