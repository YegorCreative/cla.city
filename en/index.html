Christ-Centered

Jesus Christ is the center of our church.

CLA exists to point people to Jesus Christ. Scripture is our authority, prayer shapes our life together, and we trust the Holy Spirit to transform people as the church teaches, prays, and serves.

We serve the Russian-speaking community in Los Angeles while remaining welcoming to everyone seeking Christ.
