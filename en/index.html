Who We Are

Rooted in Christ. Open to Everyone.

We are a Spirit-led, Bible-centered church serving Los Angeles. Many of us come from Russian-speaking backgrounds, and we are building one family across cultures and generations.

Whether you are returning to faith or exploring it for the first time, you are welcome here.
