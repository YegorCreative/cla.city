Why We Give

Generosity Is a Way of Life

Giving at Church LA is never about obligation or guilt. We give as an overflow of gratitude — because we've received so much from God. Every gift you make directly supports the people, programs, and spaces that make our community possible.

Where It Goes

Your Gift at Work

We steward every gift with transparency and accountability. Here's how your giving makes a difference.

🏛

General Fund

Covers Sunday services, staffing, ministry programs, and the day-to-day operations that keep our community running and growing.

🔨

Building Fund

Our facility is our home. Gifts to the building fund go toward maintenance, improvements, and our long-term goal of owning our own space.

✈️

Global Missions

We partner with missionaries and church-planting networks in Eastern Europe, Central Asia, and beyond. Your gift goes to the nations.
