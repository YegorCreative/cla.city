Your First Visit

Whether you're visiting church for the first time or searching for a church home, we want you to feel comfortable from the moment you arrive. Our team is here to welcome you and answer any questions you may have.

Parking

Free parking is available at the church campus.

What Should I Wear?

Come as you are. Some people dress formally while others dress casually. We care much more about meeting you than what you're wearing.

Children

Families are always welcome. We desire for every child to experience God's love in a safe and caring environment.

Service Information

Our Sunday service begins at 2:30 PM and lasts approximately 90 minutes. Together we worship God through music, prayer, and Biblical teaching.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions?

If you have any questions before your visit, we'd love to help. Feel free to reach out and we'll be happy to connect with you.
