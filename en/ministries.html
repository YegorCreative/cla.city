Ministry Spotlight

KidsZone

KidsZone is a fully staffed children's ministry that runs during the Sunday service. Our team creates an age-appropriate, joyful environment where children ages 0–12 can discover that God loves them and has a plan for their lives.

Every week includes age-based teaching, worship songs, games, crafts, and small-group discussion. Check-in happens at the KidsZone desk near the main entrance.

Schedule: Sundays 2:30 PM – 4:00 PM

Ministry Spotlight

Life Groups

Life Groups are small gatherings of 8–15 people who meet weekly in homes and coffee shops across greater LA. These are the places where real community happens: honest conversations, shared meals, prayer, and genuine friendship.

Groups run primarily in Russian, and cover everything from topical Bible studies to sermon follow-up discussion. New groups form regularly — ask about joining one this week.

Schedule: Various days — contact us to find your nearest group.
