Ministry Spotlight

KidsZone

KidsZone is for children ages 0–12. We want every child to feel cared for, safe, and loved by Jesus.

Children learn the Bible in age-appropriate ways, enjoy games and creativity, make friends, and grow with the church family. KidsZone is offered in connection with the Sunday gathering.

If you are visiting with children for the first time, contact us ahead of time — we would love to answer questions and help you prepare.

Ministry Spotlight

Youth Ministry

CLA Youth is for teenagers and young adults ages 13–25. It is a place to belong, build real friendships, and explore faith without pressure.

We want the next generation to grow closer to Jesus, learn to trust God, and find their place in the church community.

If you want to learn more or visit for the first time, contact us — we would love to meet you.

Ministry Spotlight

Women's Ministry

Women’s Ministry at CLA is led by Pastor Marina Malko. It is a space for women who value friendship, prayer, encouragement, and honest conversations about life with God.

We seek to strengthen one another, grow in Scripture, and help every woman feel welcomed in the church family.

If you would like to connect or learn about upcoming opportunities for women, please contact us.
