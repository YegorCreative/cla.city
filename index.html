Who We Are

A Church That Feels Like Family

We are a Spirit-led, Bible-based community rooted in Los Angeles — with deep connections to the Russian-speaking and Slavic world. Our doors are open to everyone: new believers, longtime Christians, and curious seekers. Come as you are. Leave knowing you are loved.

Every week we gather to worship, hear God's Word in English and Russian, and build real relationships that go beyond Sunday morning.
