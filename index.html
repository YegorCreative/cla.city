Our Story

A Church Built on
Real People, Real Faith

City Church LA began with a small group of Russian-speaking families who wanted a place where faith felt personal, not performative. Today, we're a growing, multicultural family — rooted in prayer, led by love, and open to everyone.

Whether you've been away from church for years or you're just exploring — you're not a stranger here. You're exactly who we were praying would walk through the door.

  • ❤️ Radical welcome
  • 📖 Scripture-rooted
  • 🌍 Multicultural family
  • 🤲 Serving our city
First Visit?

Here's What Your Sunday Looks Like

No dress code. No pressure. Just a warm room, honest worship, and people who are genuinely glad you came.

👋

A Warm Welcome

Our welcome team will greet you at the door, answer any questions, and help you feel at home from the moment you arrive.

🎵

Authentic Worship

We open with live worship — songs that stir your soul, not just fill the room. Both Russian and English songs are included.

📣

Practical Message

Our pastor teaches from the Bible in a way that speaks to real life — not just Sundays. You'll leave with something to think about.

☕

Coffee & Community

After service, stay for coffee and conversation. Some of the best moments happen right there in the lobby.

👶

Kids Are Welcome

We have safe, fun programs for children from infancy through middle school, so parents can fully engage in the service.

🌐

Bilingual Experience

Services are available in both Russian and English — because we believe everyone should hear truth in their heart language.
